Running performance and current volume

A recent race or time-trial result estimates training paces. Your current weekly mileage helps set a safe starting volume; a PB alone cannot establish that.

NEW · COLLABORATIVE VARIATIONS

Exercise variation preferences

Coach suggests a purposeful variation for each training block. You can swap it or lock your choice. Competition-lift practice stays in the programme. New variations use RPE-based load discovery, not an invented percentage of your competition 1RM.

NEW · ACCESSORY COACH

Accessory preferences

The coach rotates supporting exercises by block and tapers accessory volume near competition. Mark exercises you enjoy, want to avoid, or cannot perform. Preferred exercises can stay longer when useful.

New accessories use a rep range and RPE to establish an initial load. When you reach the top of the rep range at the target effort, consider a small increase next time.
