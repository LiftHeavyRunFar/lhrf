Slow/worsening evidence must not disappear when a timer expires. Missing evidence must not create full clearance or an indefinite event-only prohibition. 5. Does the correction retain useful work and appropriate reductions? Removing overlap-only cuts must not block a justified recovery intervention. 6. Do distinct comparable planned visits support discussion of progression while dense/catch-up/duplicate records remain limited evidence? Genuine spaced positive controls and reasonable delayed execution must remain usable. These are governance and evidence-validity choices, not proof of physiological adaptation or universal numerical thresholds.
7. Are old/new comparisons based on identical raw input bytes, source reviews and clock, with all baseline dependencies isolated from changed modules? Will inconvenient outcomes and any changes to prewritten criteria be disclosed?
8. Do generation/review/preview leave all saved state untouched until an explicit approved replacement? Existing policy must not be silently upgraded. An owner diagnostic with incomplete source identity is not the exact device programme or an installable owner package.

## Interpretation of the revised return model

The observed post-event week is a temporary proposal base, capped by reported habitual volume; it is not a newly measured habitual capacity. An isolated tolerated run cannot establish that week. Unknown return stages retain a provisional familiar-dose cap, and a new proposal date does not advance them. Conversely, an older event plus a later dated performance and at least three reported routine weeks can support preserving familiar running when no relevant adverse evidence is present. That continuity exception is not full recovery clearance or permission for additional mileage.

I consider this meaning more defensible than either an automatic twenty-one-day restoration or an unexplained indefinite recovery ceiling. The retained 20/40/60-percent and 7/21-day context conventions, evidence horizons, effort curve, conditional build rates and exact observation-link contract remain product choices; research does not establish their individual optimum. A sustained static return forecast needs a usable reassessment path, and a completed low week alone does not require maintaining a low dose forever. Same-day reports cannot establish a next-day response; later reporting must distinguish the actual observation/activity dates. This is provenance, not a universal twenty-four-hour biological safety threshold.
