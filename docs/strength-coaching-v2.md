# Strength coaching policy two

New proposals can opt into `coachingRulesVersion: 2`. Existing version-one approved prescriptions keep their role ordering, loads and effort ceilings.

Three exposures use heavy, volume and speed roles. Heavy is placed first, volume second so the existing independently calibrated variation policy can use that secondary exposure outside the competition-specific window. Two exposures use heavy and volume; one uses a strength top set plus a smaller volume dose rather than inventing extra visits. Speed work uses five doubles at a light, three-week wave, explicit acceleration cues and a stop/reduce rule when position or speed deteriorates. This is a coaching heuristic, not a measured velocity threshold or a claim that all elements of a named conjugate system have been reproduced.

For a technically established lifter with a strength-first competition goal, four final available heavy rehearsals can target singles at 8, 8.5, 8.5 and 9. RPE governs the suggestion. The final rehearsal is followed by just one easy back-off and no support work. Running-first priority caps these exposures at 8. Beginner, returning, maintenance and explicitly light policies continue to take their separate paths. The dates still come from the actual lift calendar and lift-specific recovery windows; no forced weekday is introduced.

`recoveryEvidence` can include `affectedLifts`, `status`, `repeatCount`, `baselineRpe`, `recentRpe` and `observedAt`, or per-lift objects under `lifts`. Repeated comparable overshoot or an explicit accumulating-fatigue status reduces affected main work to one triple at 6.5 plus one easy triple and removes its support work. Unaffected bench practice is preserved. This does not clear itself in a later week: an actual recovery reassessment must resolve the evidence. The proposal must surface the unresolved review gate. Generation never edits an approved programme or log.

These thresholds are product policy choices requiring the independent athlete review loop. Search tooling was unavailable during this change; no new research verification or efficacy claim is represented here. Existing research-informed repository guidance remains contextual evidence, not proof that a particular athlete is ready for the final single.

When a genuine hard set is available, the recovery suggestion uses its provisional effort-aware capacity estimate, capped by the healthy reference, instead of scaling the healthy maximum alone. Recent dated log observations take precedence; explicitly supplied `recoveryEvidence.lifts` entries can retain untimestamped weight/reps/RPE facts without creating fake workout logs. Stale or future logs and uncalibrated variations are not used for this anchor. The resulting load remains provisional and RPE still governs it.
