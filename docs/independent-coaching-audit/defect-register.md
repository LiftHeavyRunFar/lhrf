## Findings explicitly rejected or limited

- **“The engine automatically rebuilds after a bad session.”** Not reproduced. Reviews preserve source data and active prescriptions; normal changes require approval. Some proposal evidence is weak, which is a different defect.
- **“Marathon restrictions explain the owner's current programme.”** Attribution is open. The checked-in file lacks a marathon date and may not be the active reviewed snapshot.
- **“All low-volume, easy, fixed-load, maintenance or tapered weeks are undertraining.”** Unsupported without objective, context and outcomes.
- **“Every hybrid must receive three strength roles, exactly 80% easy distance, a 10% mileage ramp or a fourth-week deload.”** These are neither universal research findings nor acceptance criteria for this audit.
- **“Passing all tests or producing a feasible week demonstrates efficacy.”** Software correctness and physiological benefit are separate claims.
