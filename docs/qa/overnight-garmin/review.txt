B1 diagnosis: source findings versus physical evidence
1. Existing LHRF sends native Intervals description steps, target PACE, Run workout,
   local calendar date and external_id via events/bulk?upsert=true. This is a
   structured-workout route, not an activity upload. It retains array order and
   absolute secs/km targets; its existing response validator checks parsed pace
   bounds and distances. Source/mocks prove representation; they do not prove
   that a particular live Intervals account parsed or forwarded it correctly.
2. Explicit work/recovery roles were discarded before serialization. Labels alone
   left intensity to parser heuristics, and equal-distance/equal-pace steps could
   be reversed without the response validator noticing. This defect is reproduced
   against the exact baseline module. The fix serializes active/rest/warmup/
   cooldown explicitly and validates the returned intensity at each position.
3. Exact min/km ranges and all distances/recoveries are retained. Malformed or
   non-pace targets now fail instead of accepting an embedded mm:ss substring.
   Decimal distances avoid scientific-notation ambiguity; numeric preservation
   is checked to 1e-9km and parsed confirmation to0.1m /1sec per km. No interval,
   pace, recovery or coaching prescription is simplified. Time-only or mixed
   time/distance steps remain unsupported with an actionable explanation. Legacy
   text-based timed repeats cannot export estimated distance approximations.
4. Baseline identity included the user-selected export date. Moving quality:0
   from2026-11-07 to2026-11-08 changed lhrf-run-v1:quality:0:2026-11-07 to
   lhrf-run-v1:quality:0:2026-11-08, creating another event. Now identity is based
   on the original prescribed occurrence date, while start_date_local follows
   the selected export date. Weeks remain distinct; component/preview/tools
   paths normalize the same occurrence; multiple runs retain separate indices.
5. The Forerunner165 manual documents receiving Connect workouts. Intervals
   documents onward Garmin upload, but physical receipt/execution on this owner's
   account/watch is NOT verified. API success never confirms watch delivery.
6. Manual actions: existing one-time Intervals Garmin upload authorization/Run
   filter/threshold pace setup; select date and confirm LHRF send; sync Connect;
   inspect the actual watch workout. Upload horizon is normally the next week,
   subject to current account settings. Garmin may show third-party workouts as
   formatted text rather than an editable native workout; this alone is not
   proof of lost structure. If needed, use Connect's Send to Device action.
7. The real owner failure cannot conclusively be assigned without account/watch
   evidence. LHRF defects above are demonstrated. A successful structured receipt
   plus missing Connect workout points to Intervals upload settings/export or
   Garmin ingestion; Connect present/watch absent points to device sync; watch
   steps without targets despite parsed pace points to downstream pace setup or
   export handling. The checklist separates these stages without guessing.
