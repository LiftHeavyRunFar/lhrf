## Genuine shortcomings and separate-review priorities

1. **High — Stage-1 scheduling regression, O1 and O3.** Clean pre-Stage-1 output has M/W/F lifting; corrected output clusters M/T/W. Across the full horizon there are **11 adjacent-day pairs of lower-body sessions at RPE ≥8 for O1, and 29 for O3**, versus zero in both original and clean pre-Stage-1 output. These counts describe programmed pairs, not observed injuries. Consecutive training can be defensible with appropriate dose and evidence, but here the allocator also places RPE-9 deadlift and squat on adjacent days, ignores available spacing, and supplies no individual rationale. Review an allocation preference for recovery/weekly spread while preserving feasible visits, fixed athlete requirements and Taylor's approved improvement. Do not simply force a universal rest-day rule. No correction is implemented here.
2. **Moderate — unresolved marathon readiness, especially O1's four-hour peak run.** This is visible uncertainty, not false feasibility or a newly introduced prescription regression. Confirm current longitudinal mileage, long-run tolerance, fueling and recovery before authorising the future build. Review the duration/support-distribution tradeoff rather than automatically adding kilometres. Both marathon plans remain provisional.
3. **Lower — event-specificity during the taper.** The original-three running taper remains easy-only for three weeks. Small familiar race-pace reminders may improve preparation once recovery is established. This was not changed by Stages 1–3; a deliberate conservative taper can be acceptable under the missing-history conditions.

The original-three facts do not contain persistent-fatigue evidence or a hard race-duration cutoff, and O1's seven-day post-meet interval already has light lifting. Therefore these three alone cannot demonstrate all Stage-3 safety corrections. The unchanged five-athlete safeguard replay supplies that targeted evidence: Keiko's actual hard time conflict stays blocked, fatigue affects maintenance lifting, and Idris remains light within the partial recovery week. No synthetic original-three inputs were altered to manufacture those cases.
