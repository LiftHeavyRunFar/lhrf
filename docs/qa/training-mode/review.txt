Problem and result
Start previously retained the large browsing masthead and largely unchanged
card hierarchy. Historical comparison chose the newest compatible exercise record,
so Tuesday volume bench could displace the previous Friday heavy session.

Active Training Mode now hides the oversized masthead only on the live training
screen, with a compact session/purpose/progress header, angular electric-blue
exercise heading, clear numeric prescription/entry fields and an emphasized open
exercise card. Other and completed cards remain readable and accessible. Existing
exercise navigation, correction and set-completion controls remain. Leave & resume
returns to browsing with a visible Resume training action; completion restores the
normal browsing/finished identity. Preview retains the original browsing identity
and includes historical references before Start.

Goldman Bold is the licensed compatible angular face, locally bundled and cached
under SIL OFL 1.1. Copyright/licence/source are in assets/fonts. It is not claimed
to be the unknown original font embedded in the raster logo. Headings cap at 30px
(22px at 320px), with restrained metallic-blue text and no added animation/glow.
Numerical fields remain standard editable number inputs with larger tabular type,
accessible names and completion state; 320px spacing accommodates decimal loads.

Presentation is a separate helper and stylesheet. The only new remembered value
is a tab-scoped exercise index in sessionStorage, keyed by existing workout ID and
start timestamp. Athlete storage, draft fields and completed-workout schema are
unchanged. Rest code, controls, persistence and timing are untouched. There are
no automatic rest prompts, new timers, blocking overlays or rigid set sequencing.
The original final-set collapse behavior is preserved; the initial regression
caught a view-restoration interaction and was corrected without weakening its test.

Purpose matching and evidence
The pure read-only history layer first retains compatible exercise/variation and
load/setup conventions and excludes unsuitable records. Among these candidates it
prioritizes the same named training purpose, then the actual latest recorded date.
Heavy, volume, speed/technique and other existing defined roles are read from
trainingPurpose/trainingRole/benchRole. Top/backoff/straight are SET roles and are
never treated as purpose. No purpose is inferred from weights, reps, RPE, weekday,
Day 3 of 5, numeric programme-week equality or a fresh coaching-engine replay.

Evidence comes from recorded set/session metadata, saved completed-workout
prescriptions and preserved dated programme snapshots. Original installation
archives containing a saved schedule/week and Stage 8 full calendar archives are
supported. Archive week offsets are used only to resolve their recorded calendar
structure, not to equate different programmes' week identities. Linked legacy sets
with timestamps differing from their completed workout can recover the saved
purpose using that historical link and actual day. Current session IDs never
need to equal historical session IDs. Conflicting evidence stays uncertain.

Each relevant exercise card includes its own compact history summary: recorded
top sets and backoffs, date and original loads/reps/RPE/roles. Identical recorded
sets are compacted only for summary display; All recorded sets expands every
suitable original row. Rep differences are explicitly labelled, not converted to
an equivalent prescription. Comparison details keep setup uncertainty and evidence
source available. Without a reliable same-purpose match, alternatives are clearly
labelled or no reliable comparison is shown. Context never supplies a new load.

Limitations and release route
Missing or contradictory historical purpose remains unknown; the app does not
reclassify old data or reconstruct a prescription from current policy. General
programme intent alone cannot prove a particular historical exposure's purpose.
Unknown legacy equipment still carries the existing warning. Same-day records
remain excluded as before. The UI does not certify the actual owner's 30 October
values; it reads their preserved local records at runtime.

Goldman is a compatible licensed choice, not recovered original logo lettering.
Final visual acceptance, physical Android keyboard/font scaling/TalkBack and
native app-switch/back-gesture behavior remain for owner/Ella review. Automated
Garmin mocks do not confirm watch delivery; the prior Forerunner 165 checklist
remains applicable. No coaching-policy decision was required or changed.
